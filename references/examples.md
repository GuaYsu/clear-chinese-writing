# Style calibration examples

Use these examples only when style calibration is useful. Do not copy their subject matter into unrelated tasks.

## Imagined contrast

Less natural:
“本研究关注的并非简单的数据处理，而是对实验规律的深入分析。”

Better:
“本文对实验数据进行拟合，并比较不同参数条件下的变化规律。”

Reason: the first version invents “简单的数据处理” as an opponent. The second states the actual work.

## Generic limitation

Less useful:
“本实验所得结果不能完全代表实际工业条件下的情况。”

Better when supported:
“实验在常压、20–60 ℃范围内进行。工业反应器的压力和传热条件不同，因此该趋势向工业条件外推时仍需验证。”

Reason: the limitation identifies the boundary and its cause.

## Nominalization

Less direct:
“对实验结果进行了进一步的分析。”

Better:
“进一步分析了实验结果。”

Note: prefer natural Chinese over the mechanically shortest possible form. In some contexts, “进一步分析实验结果” is appropriate; in others, the finite form above reads more smoothly.

## Evaluation without evidence

Less informative:
“该方法显著提高了计算精度。”

Better when data are available:
“平均相对误差由 5.4% 降至 1.1%。”

## Artificial escalation

Less natural:
“该方法不仅提高了计算效率，更为后续研究奠定了坚实基础。”

Better when both claims are real:
“该方法将单次计算时间由 12 s 降至 4 s。优化后的算法随后用于参数扫描。”

## Mechanical metadiscourse

Less direct:
“值得注意的是，当网格数增加至 200 后，误差下降幅度明显减小。”

Better:
“网格数超过 200 后，继续加密网格带来的误差下降已经很小。”

## Over-compression

Stiff in ordinary discussion:
“STE 的出发点更稳。”

More natural:
“相比之下，以 STE 作为出发点会更稳妥一些。”

More informative when the reason matters:
“相比之下，我更倾向于从 STE 出发，因为它约束的是表达本身，受模型口癖变化的影响也更小。”

Reason: the short version removes the comparison frame and compresses “稳妥” into vague shorthand. The longer versions restore the relation without adding empty padding.

## Register discontinuity

Less coherent:
“我觉得这个方向挺有意思。STE 的出发点更稳。该方案可降低风格漂移风险。”

Better for discussion:
“我觉得这个方向挺有意思。相比之下，我还是更倾向于从 STE 出发，因为它约束的是表达本身，受模型口癖变化的影响也更小。”

Better for formal prose:
“相比其他以表面语言特征为主的规则，STE 更关注表达的准确性和一致性，因此更适合作为底层写作规范。”

Reason: choose a register and maintain it instead of shifting sentence by sentence.

## Paragraph density

Avoid formatting a formal report as a chain of isolated sentences separated by blank lines. Keep related sentences together when they develop one observation or argument.

## Legitimate negation

Do not ban contrast mechanically. This is natural when a real distinction matters:
“相关性不能直接证明因果关系。”

This sentence prevents a plausible and consequential inference, so the negation is informative.
